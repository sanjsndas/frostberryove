Frequently asked questions

Do you test all your recipes? +
Yes — every recipe is tested at least twice in my home kitchen, and many are tested by volunteer readers before publication. I note any quirks or substitutions in the headnotes.
Can I substitute ingredients? +
Usually, yes. I include substitution notes where I've tested them. For baking, weight measurements are strongly recommended for accuracy.
Why "Frostberry Oven Lane"? +
Frostberry is the name of the wild blackberry that grows along the lane behind my house. It felt right for a blog about using what's around you.
How can I work with you? +
I'm open to select brand partnerships and freelance writing. Please visit the contact page for details.
