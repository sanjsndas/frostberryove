Cookie Policy

Last updated: March 12, 2025  ·  Effective: March 12, 2025

At Frostberry Oven Lane (accessible via frostberryovenlane.com), we use cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your browsing experience, analyse site traffic, and understand where our visitors come from. This Cookie Policy explains what cookies are, how we use them, and how you can manage your preferences.

For more information about how we handle your personal data, please see our Privacy Policy.

🍪 The short version: We use a few essential cookies to make the site work, plus some analytics cookies to see which recipes are popular. We don't use advertising cookies to track you across the internet. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, as well as to provide information to the site owners.

Cookies can be "persistent" or "session" cookies. Persistent cookies remain on your device after you close your browser, while session cookies are deleted as soon as you close your browser.

2. How we use cookies

We use cookies for the following purposes:

  • Essential cookies: These are necessary for the website to function properly. They enable core functionality such as page navigation and access to secure areas. The website cannot function properly without these cookies.
  • Analytics cookies: These help us understand how visitors interact with our website by collecting and reporting information anonymously. This helps us improve our recipes and content.
  • Preference cookies: These allow our website to remember choices you make (such as your preferred language or the region you are in) and provide enhanced, more personal features.

Below is a detailed list of the cookies we use on Frostberry Oven Lane:

4. Third-party cookies

Some cookies are placed by third-party services that appear on our pages. We do not control these cookies. Currently, we use:

  • Google Analytics: To analyse traffic and usage patterns. You can learn more about Google's privacy practices and opt-out at Google Analytics Opt-out.
  • Embedded content: Occasionally we embed recipes or videos from other platforms (e.g., YouTube, Instagram). These platforms may set their own cookies. We recommend reviewing their cookie policies.

5. Managing your cookie preferences

You have the right to decide whether to accept or reject cookies. You can exercise your cookie preferences by:

  • Browser settings: Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can set your browser to refuse all cookies or to indicate when a cookie is being sent. Below are links to guidance for common browsers:
    • Google Chrome
    • Mozilla Firefox
    • Safari
    • Microsoft Edge
  • Opt-out tools: You can opt out of Google Analytics across all websites by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.
  • Consent banner: When you first visit our site, you will see a cookie consent banner where you can accept or decline non-essential cookies. You can change your choice at any time by clearing your browser cookies and revisiting the site, or by using the preference link in the footer (if available).

Please note that if you disable cookies, some parts of our website may not function properly, and your experience may be affected.

6. Do Not Track signals

Some browsers have a "Do Not Track" (DNT) feature that lets you tell websites that you do not want to have your online activities tracked. We currently do not respond to DNT signals, as there is no common industry standard for how to interpret them. However, we do not track you across third-party websites for advertising purposes.

7. Changes to this Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology or legislation. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically to stay informed about our use of cookies.
